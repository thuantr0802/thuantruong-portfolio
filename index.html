01 | Customs Procedures

Export Customs Clearance: Vietnam to Korea

A full customs clearance case for a Vietnamese cashew exporter shipping to Korea. The case follows the shipment from contract preparation to customs declaration, showing how HS classification, invoice value, origin proof, plant health control, fumigation, sea transport documents, and VNACCS data have to align before the cargo can move.

Key pointThis project is about the operating logic behind export clearance. It shows how one cashew shipment becomes compliant through a complete document chain: commercial records, transport proof, origin certification, health and fumigation certificates, and electronic customs declaration.
