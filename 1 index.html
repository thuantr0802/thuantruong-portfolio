CASHERA — Vietnamese Cashews to the UK Market

Go-to-market business plan | Chapter 6 Lead

A full go-to-market plan for CASHERA, a premium Vietnamese cashew brand entering the UK market. The project covered TAM/SAM/SOM analysis, a 5-year revenue forecast, pricing strategy, and a complete IMC plan spanning Amazon UK, social media, and food exhibitions.

Danisa Rainbow Cookies — Limited Edition Tet Campaign

Product concept & positioning | Group project

A go-to-market concept for a limited-edition Tet product under the Danisa brand, targeting middle-to-high income Vietnamese consumers. The campaign centred on a "7 colors, 7 flavors" rainbow theme, positioning the tin as a premium cultural gift — not just a cookie, but a symbol of gratitude and family connection during Lunar New Year.

My Contribution

Structured the strategic rationale for choosing Danisa — including Vietnam's 1.33B USD cookie market and Tet's 54% share of confectionery sales. Shaped the brand positioning section, framing Rainbow Cookies as a cultural symbol rather than a product extension. Refined and presented both sections to align with the campaign brief.
